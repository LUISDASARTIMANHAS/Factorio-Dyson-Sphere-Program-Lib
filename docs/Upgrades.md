Upgrades
From the Dyson Sphere Program Wiki

The many upgrades in Dyson Sphere Program can be used throughout the game to improve Icarus's abilities, speed up production lines, optimize logistics, and increase the Dyson sphere's effectiveness. Below are the upgrades and their appropriate costs.

Completing all upgrades (exluding repeatables and those requiring items other than matricies) requires:

137.41k 139.0k 128.4k 96.8k 60.5k 90.8k
Note: Upgrades are sorted by how they appear from top to bottom and by tier.

These upgrades highlight where resources are located on a planet and eventually show the player what resources are located in other star systems. As such, these are mostly quality-of-life upgrades.
